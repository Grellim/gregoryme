# Vagão do Café — Owner & Operator

Owning a coffee shop isn't really about coffee. It's about creating a space where people feel like they belong.

Vagão do Café was my answer to the question: what does it take to make someone walk in as a stranger and leave as a regular? The answer turned out to be everything — the lighting, the music, the way you remember their order, the extra 5 seconds of eye contact that says "I see you."

I ran every aspect of the business. Mornings were about dialing in the espresso and greeting the early crowd. Afternoons were supply runs, finances, social media. Evenings were deep-cleaning and planning tomorrow's special.

The lesson that stays with me: hospitality isn't a technique. It's attention turned into action.

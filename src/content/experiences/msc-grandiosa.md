# MSC Grandiosa — Guest Service Specialist

The MSC Grandiosa is one of the largest cruise ships in the world — 331m long, 19 decks, carrying over 6,300 passengers. Working here means constant motion, constant pressure, and a clientele that expects perfection.

As a Guest Service Specialist, I was the human face of the ship when things went wrong. Lost luggage, missed excursions, dietary emergencies, cabin issues — my desk was where frustration turned into resolution.

The ship operates 24/7 across multiple languages and cultures. I learned to read tension before it surfaced, to de-escalate with presence rather than script, and to make people feel genuinely cared for in a space where they could easily feel like just another passenger number.

# Princess Cruises — Guest Services Coordinator

If MSC taught me volume, Princess taught me depth. Longer voyages, older clientele, more complex needs.

My role bridged the front desk and the engine room of guest services. I coordinated special requests — dietary, medical, celebratory — and made sure nothing fell through the cracks across weeks at sea.

I trained junior staff on something no manual teaches: how to listen for what a guest isn't saying. The elderly couple who won't admit they're overwhelmed. The solo traveler who's lonely but too proud to join activities. The family that needs help but doesn't want to be a burden.

Those are the moments hospitality becomes something more.

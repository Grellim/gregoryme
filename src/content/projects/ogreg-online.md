# ogreg.online — Personal Brand Website

The brief was simple: "I want a website that feels like me." But "feeling like Gregory" is a nuanced thing — it's warmth without sentimentality, professionalism without coldness, energy without chaos.

Every design decision maps to a feeling. The accent color (#E8632A) is burnt amber — warm like a sunset, energetic like a spark. The typography is Space Grotesk — modern, geometric, confident. The interactions are scroll-driven because Gregory's story deserves to unfold, not just be listed.

The CMS means Gregory can update his own content without touching code. The feeling words per experience make sure the site isn't just a resume — it's an emotional resume.

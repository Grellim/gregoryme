# Hospitality Management & Leadership — ESSEC via Coursera

A deep dive into the theory and practice of world-class hospitality, from the fundamentals of service design to the complexities of managing multicultural teams in high-pressure environments.

Key takeaways: the difference between service (transactional) and hospitality (emotional), frameworks for designing memorable guest experiences, and leadership strategies for maintaining team morale during extended operational periods — directly applicable to life aboard cruise ships.
